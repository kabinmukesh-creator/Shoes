Who we are

A shop, not a warehouse. We carry what we can stand behind, and we know where every pair was lasted.

Frank Rowan opened the door in 1974 with three brands and a Singer patcher in the back. His granddaughter Alice runs the floor now. The patcher is still back there — it has outlasted two tills and one flood.

We stock around ninety styles at a time. That is deliberate. Every one has been worn by someone on staff for at least a fortnight before it reaches the wall, and if it creases badly, rubs at the heel, or the outsole hardens in the cold, it does not reach the wall at all.

  • Both feet measured on a Brannock, every visit
  • Thirty-day wear-in return, worn or not
  • Resoling and stitch repair on anything welted
  • Cleaning and conditioning clinic, first Saturday monthly
